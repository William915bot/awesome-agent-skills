# Instagram Reels Growth

Create practical Instagram content that sounds like the account owner, earns attention quickly, and gives viewers a reason to save, share, comment, or follow.

## Establish the positioning

Use positioning supplied in the current request. If none is supplied and the user is William, default to:

- Core identity: marine supply representative in Kaohsiung; ship service, vessel supply, gas detection, and marine chemicals.
- Supporting themes: business growth, work observations, AI productivity, broad market notes, and selective personal stories.
- Content mix: 50% marine work and expertise, 20% sales/career growth, 15% AI and productivity, 10% crypto/U.S./Taiwan market notes, 5% personal life and values.
- Voice: concise, sincere, observant, practical, and conversational; avoid exaggerated guru language.

Ask one concise question only when a missing detail would materially change the result. Otherwise state the assumption and proceed.

## Select the requested output

### Topic bank

For a request for ideas or 30 topics, provide exactly 30 distinct Reels concepts. For each include:

1. Topic/title
2. Viewer value or angle
3. Hook
4. Recommended format when useful: talking head, POV, B-roll, screen recording, before/after, or list

Favor topics that viewers would save as a reference or share with a colleague. Avoid near-duplicate angles.

### Hook set

For a Hook request, write exactly 20 openings unless the user gives another number. Keep every Chinese Hook within 15 Chinese characters, excluding punctuation where practical.

Vary the mechanism across curiosity gap, counterintuitive claim, specific mistake, immediate benefit, relatable tension, and first-person discovery. Do not use false urgency, unsupported guarantees, or empty phrases such as “你一定要知道.”

### Complete Reels script

Provide:

- Goal and target viewer
- Best Hook
- Spoken script in the creator's natural voice
- Shot-by-shot filming direction
- On-screen subtitles
- CTA
- Approximate duration

Make the first useful point arrive immediately. Use spoken Traditional Chinese for William unless another language is requested. Keep claims factual and mark any detail that requires verification.

### Eight-second Reel

Design a realistic video that can be shot on a phone. Use this timing structure unless the concept requires a small adjustment:

- 0–2 seconds: Hook and visual interruption
- 2–6 seconds: one clear insight, contrast, or reveal
- 6–8 seconds: CTA

Include Hook, timestamped subtitles, shot directions, and one low-friction CTA. Do not overload the screen; target one short subtitle idea per shot.

### Caption

Write a readable, story-led Instagram caption that preserves the user's voice:

1. Open with a concrete moment or tension.
2. Use short paragraphs and natural transitions.
3. Deliver a useful observation or takeaway.
4. End with one relevant interaction prompt.

Avoid generic motivational filler, excessive emojis, overly polished corporate prose, and AI-like summary phrases. Use no more than 3–5 relevant hashtags unless the user requests a hashtag strategy.

### Thirty-day plan

Provide exactly 30 days in a compact table with:

| Day | Pillar | Reels topic | Hook | Filming direction | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Distribute pillars according to the selected positioning rather than grouping all similar posts together. Build narrative continuity: introduce the creator, establish expertise, reveal process, answer objections, share proof or lessons, and deepen audience connection.

## Quality checks

Before delivering, verify:

- The Hook matches the actual payoff.
- The content is specific enough to film without another planning round.
- The creator can plausibly say the lines aloud.
- Each CTA asks for only one action.
- Technical, financial, or regulatory claims are not invented.
- The output respects the exact requested count and length limit.
- Repeated ideas have meaningfully different angles.

When the user says only “把這個做成 Reels,” infer the strongest format and deliver the complete script, an 8-second cut, and a caption together.
